Overload engine

Normal / Overload Peak — compares your last non-deload session against its targets.

▲ Hit every planned set at target weight & reps → +2.5 kg / +5 lb (weight mode) or +1 rep (reps mode, or bodyweight).

= Missed / stalled → hold the same weight and target reps.

▼ Deload / Rest → 85% of previous weight and 60% of previous volume (fewer sets, fewer reps). Hatched rows are optional.

↺ After a deload, recommendations resume from your last normal session.
